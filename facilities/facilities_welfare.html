Our Commitment to You

Welfare Measures

A holistic suite of benefits designed to support every dimension of our faculty and staff's professional and personal lives.

Financial Support

Comprehensive financial security programs ensuring every employee is protected and incentivized for outstanding contributions.

  • Employee Provident Fund (EPF) for all permanent staff
  • ESIC health insurance scheme coverage
  • Festival Advance facility during major festivals
  • Fee concession for children of staff members
  • Incentives for research publications & patents
  • Performance-based salary increments

Facilities & Amenities

Day-to-day conveniences that make campus life comfortable, productive, and enjoyable for every team member.

  • Free transportation across 30+ routes in Vellore
  • Complimentary food on extra-working days
  • Individual computing system for each faculty
  • High-speed internet & Wi-Fi across campus
  • Modern air-conditioned staff rooms
  • Dedicated parking facilities

Career Progression

A culture dedicated to continuous learning, professional development, and opportunities for academic advancement.

  • Funded Faculty Development Programs (FDPs)

  • Promotion based on academic achievements

Institutional Policy

Benefit Policy Details

Transparent policies ensuring every employee is fully informed of their entitlements as per government norms and institutional guidelines.

EPF & ESIC

  • Employee Provident Fund for all permanent staff as per Government norms
  • ESIC health insurance with IN-PATIENT & OUT-PATIENT coverage
  • 100% statutory compliance — no delays in remittance

Ward Academic Fee Concession

  • 25% fee waiver for children of staff from day one
  • Increases to 40% after 2 continuous years of service
  • Applicable to B.E. / B.Tech / MBA programs at Kingston

Festival Advance

  • Advance facility during major festivals (Diwali, Pongal, Eid, etc.)
  • Repayment in 6 equal monthly installments
  • Salary advance also available for personal emergencies

On Duty & Deputation

  • On Duty (OD) granted for official academic/research events
  • Official deputation as per Institution norms
  • Travel reimbursement for approved conferences & FDPs

Contingency Grant

  • Contingency grant provided for teaching faculty for research consumables
  • Seed funding available for new research projects
  • Publication incentive bonus for indexed journal papers

Gratuity & Service Benefits

  • Gratuity paid as per Payment of Gratuity Act
  • Transparent HR policies with grievance redressal cell
  • Salary credited on time — no delays, ever
