Library Advisory Committee

Oversees collection development, digital journals, DELNET subscriptions, and procurement of text and reference volumes.

Maintenance Committee

Monitors campus physical infrastructure, computing clusters, electrical substations, water treatment, and campus hygiene.

Staff Selection Committee

Coordinates faculty recruitment, expert interview panels, qualification verification, and promotion evaluations.

Transport Committee

Manages fleet operations across 25+ bus routes connecting Vellore, Ranipet, Arcot, and surrounding districts.

Rotaract Club

Fosters community service, blood donation camps, social leadership, and youth community development activities.

Green Energy Club

Promotes campus sustainability, solar awareness, environmental sapling drives, and zero-plastic campaigns.

Fine Arts & Cultural Club

Organizes annual cultural fests, theatre, singing, classical dance, and talent competitions across branches.

Science & Maths Club

Conducts science exhibits, mathematical puzzle challenges, Pi-Day celebrations, and aptitude training.

Women Empowerment Cell (WEC)

Empowers female students and faculty through health awareness workshops, legal literacy, and entrepreneurship mentoring.

Industry Institute Partnership (IIPC)

Facilitates joint consultancy projects, corporate industrial visits, guest lectures, and corporate internship MoUs.

Youth Red Cross (YRC) & NSS

Encourages civic responsibility, disaster preparedness training, community health outreach, and rural literacy camps.

Institution Innovation Council (IIC)

Ministry of Education approved innovation hub fostering ideation, hackathons, intellectual property patents, and startups.
