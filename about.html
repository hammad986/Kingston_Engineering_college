About Kingston Engineering College

Kingston Engineering College

Kingston Engineering College, located in the sprawling campus of 11.62 acres in Vellore City, on the Chittoor National Highway, is situated just 5 km from Katpadi Railway Station. It was established under the flagship of M/s. Duraimurugan Educational Trust, approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and affiliated with Anna University, Chennai.

Kingston Engineering College has ventured into the field of technical education with the vision of imparting knowledge, technical expertise, professional competence, and strong ethical values to students. The institution prepares graduates to meet rapidly evolving technological advancements, address the changing needs of society, and contribute meaningfully to Engineering and Technology.

The mission of the institution is to provide an excellent environment for teaching, learning, innovation, and research that reflects the highest standards of technical education. Kingston Engineering College has developed a strong academic ecosystem supported by quality infrastructure, modern laboratories, experienced faculty, and committed management to achieve this objective.

The learning experience at Kingston Engineering College offers students an inspiring academic journey enriched by a multicultural campus environment, collaborative learning, research opportunities, and continuous pursuit of academic excellence and professional growth.
