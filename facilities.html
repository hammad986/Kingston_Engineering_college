Auditorium

Kingston Engineering College is home to a state-of-the-art auditorium that serves as the central hub for cultural, academic, and ceremonial events across the campus. The auditorium is designed with world-class acoustics, modern lighting systems, and an elegant interior that creates an impressive atmosphere for all occasions.

With a seating capacity of over 1,200 people, the main auditorium is equipped with advanced audio-visual systems, LED projection screens, and a fully furnished stage that can accommodate large-scale performances, seminars, conferences, and convocation ceremonies. The infrastructure meets international standards ensuring a premium experience for every event.

The facility is also equipped with modern climate control systems, high-speed internet connectivity, and professional stage management equipment, making it ideal for both formal academic events and vibrant cultural celebrations. Our dedicated team of technicians ensures every event is executed flawlessly.

Kingston Engineering College has always been a proud promoter of sports and athletic excellence, offering world-class sporting facilities to nurture talent and build holistic development among students. The sprawling sports complex is equipped with international-standard courts and fields for cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, badminton, table tennis, kabaddi, kho-kho, athletics, and many other indoor and outdoor sports disciplines. The college actively encourages participation in inter-collegiate, state, and national level competitions, with over 85 students competing across 18 sporting disciplines at the Anna University Zonal Sports Meet 2023 alone.

Kingston Engineering College was crowned the Overall Champion at the 2023 Anna University Zonal Sports Meet, securing 12 Gold, 8 Silver, and 15 Bronze medals — the Best Performance Trophy among 45+ affiliated colleges. The college also emerged as Overall Winners of the Inter-Collegiate Sports Meet 2023 for the fifth consecutive year, amassing 248 points against top institutions including VIT Vellore (212) and SRM Chennai (196). Championship titles were won in Cricket, Basketball (Men), Volleyball, Badminton, Athletics, and Kabaddi, with 25 students qualifying for the Inter-Zone Championship. The men's 4×100m relay team set a new meet record of 42.3 seconds.

Under the guidance of qualified sports coaches and the Director of Physical Education, Kingston athletes receive comprehensive support including fully funded travel and accommodation, diet plans, sports psychology sessions, modern equipment, and uniforms provided by the college. Sports students are eligible for academic relaxations including a minimum 60% attendance requirement for Anna University examinations, rescheduling of internal assessment tests during sports participation, and tuition fee relaxation for winners at zonal, university, state, and national levels. Regular yoga sessions, fitness camps, and wellness programs are conducted throughout the academic year.

Kingston Engineering College plans to continue its legacy of sporting excellence with the construction of a new indoor stadium and synthetic athletic track. Winners are honored annually at the college Sports Day with awards, certificates, and enhanced sports scholarships. The college's commitment to sporting excellence alongside academic achievement has made Kingston a recognized powerhouse in university-level sports across Tamil Nadu.
