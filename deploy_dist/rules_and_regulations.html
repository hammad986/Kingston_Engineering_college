Institutional Discipline • Student Code of Conduct

Rules and Regulations

To cultivate an atmosphere of academic rigor, mutual respect, and professional ethics, all students of Kingston Engineering College are expected to adhere strictly to campus conduct regulations.

  • Home
  • Academics
  • Rules & Regulations

College Timings

College instructional hours are strictly from 9:00 A.M. to 04:30 P.M. Students are not permitted to enter into the campus after 9:00 A.M. and are also not allowed to leave the college premises before 04:30 P.M.

  • Punctuality is strictly enforced at campus gates and during class transitions.
  • During instructional hours, visitors and outside guests are strictly not entertained to meet students without prior administrative authorization.
  • Day scholars arriving by college bus must board at their designated stops punctually.

Campus Dress Code

All students inside the college campus must be neatly and professionally attired reflecting an academic environment:

  • For Male Students: Expected to wear self-coloured formal trousers with formal shirts neatly tucked in. Formal shoes are mandatory; slippers, casual footwear, and sports shoes are strictly prohibited.
  • For Female Students: Must wear decent Salwar Kameez with a V-shaped dupatta neatly pinned on either side of the shoulders.
  • Lab Sessions: Prescribed departmental lab coats / protective gear must be worn during all workshop and laboratory cycles.

Identity Card & Conduct

Each student is issued an official photo Identity Card. The card must be visibly worn around the neck at all times inside the campus and while commuting on college buses.

Mandatory Campus Discipline Norms:
  • Smoking or possession of any tobacco, alcohol, or narcotic products is strictly prohibited throughout the campus.
  • Use and possession of cell phones inside classrooms and academic corridors is strictly prohibited. Confiscation and disciplinary action apply.
  • Students are advised not to wear costly gold jewellery or bring excessive cash into the campus.

Leave & Attendance Policy

Regular attendance of a minimum 75% is mandatory for semester examination eligibility according to Anna University norms:

  • Prior permission letters signed by parents/guardians must be submitted to the Head of Department before any anticipated leave.
  • For medical leave, an authentic Registered Medical Practitioner's certificate along with prescription must be produced upon return.
  • Habitual absenteeism will result in intimation to parents and necessary disciplinary inquiry.
