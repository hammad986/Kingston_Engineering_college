ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

MBAs are very productive and play a vital role in producing a large number of industry captains, wealth creators, renowned entrepreneurs, top-rated management academicians and leaders spearheading social causes and working with the underprivileged and the needy.

The program's main objective is to develop young men and women into competent professional managers, capable of working in any sector of organized activity, proceeding leadership and achieving excellence in performance while contributing to the welfare of the larger society. The program equips students with the required conceptual and interpersonal skills and sense of social purpose for managerial decision-making, develops leadership capabilities to act as change agents, and nurtures the desire to excel in performance without compromising integrity, honesty and fairness.

The Department has excellent infrastructure including an air-conditioned seminar hall equipped with LCD projectors and computer with internet facility. Students have access to numerous books, Management Journals and Magazines in the library. Dedicated campus-wide computer with internet facility provides every student the opportunity to stay in touch with the latest in the information age.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Industry-Ready Curriculum

Leadership Development

PROGRAM OBJECTIVES

  • To develop young men and women into competent professional managers capable of working in any sector.
  • To equip students with conceptual and interpersonal skills for managerial decision-making.
  • To develop leadership capabilities to act as change agents and sources of motivation.
  • To nurture the desire to excel in performance without compromising integrity.
  • To help students become top-quality decision makers with conceptual foundations and social skills.
