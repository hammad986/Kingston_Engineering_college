VISION & MISSION

VISION OF THE DEPARTMENT

To build competent industry-ready professionals to largely contribute to society by imparting knowledge in the domain of Computer Science and Engineering with managerial skills, human and social values.

MISSION OF THE DEPARTMENT

M1

Fundamental Excellence

Educating students with the background of fundamentals, stretching their imagination to meet the challenges in the field of Computer Science and Engineering.

M2

Technology Transformation

Transforming emerging technology to young minds via effective teaching-learning processes.

M3

Holistic Development

Imparting self-learning ability, leadership, and managerial quality to the students.
