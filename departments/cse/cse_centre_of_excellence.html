CENTRE OF EXCELLENCE

INDUSTRY COLLABORATED CENTRES OF EXCELLENCE

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering has established three Centres of Excellence (CoEs) in collaboration with leading industry partners. These CoEs provide specialized training and industry-mentored, real-time projects in emerging technologies, equipping students with the skills and experience needed to become industry-ready professionals.

Cloud Computing

Industry Partner: AtoS

Focuses on developing skills in cloud-based systems including Cloud Engineers, Cloud Architects, and Cloud Security Specialists. Students gain hands-on experience with cloud platforms and infrastructure management.

Full Stack Computing

Industry Partner: NTT Data

Trains students in both front-end and back-end web development, covering all aspects of building and deploying web applications. Includes modern frameworks, databases, and DevOps practices.

Cyber Security

Industry Partner: LTIMindtree

Addresses cyber threats through training in Security Architecture, Cybersecurity Engineering, Information Security Analysis, and Penetration Testing. Includes hands-on labs and certification prep.

MSC TECHNOLOGY COLLABORATION

The department also has a collaboration with MSC Technology India Pvt Ltd which provided 25 latest computer system CPUs to set up a skill development centre. This partnership includes guest lectures, industry visits, and campus placement facilitation for software engineering departments.
