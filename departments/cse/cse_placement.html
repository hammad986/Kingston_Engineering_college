PLACEMENT

PLACEMENT & TRAINING OVERVIEW

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering has a dedicated Placement and Training (PAT) cell that has successfully placed 5,000+ engineers and MBA graduates over the last 13 years. The training program begins from the first year itself, focusing on verbal communication, aptitude, and problem-solving skills, followed by technical skill transformation in the third year.

The college has signed MoUs with more than 50 industries, providing students with ample opportunities for internships, training, and final placements.

KEY PLACEMENT HIGHLIGHTS

50+ Industry MoUs
10 Lakhs Highest Package
13+ Years of Excellence

TRAINING PROGRAM

Year 1

Foundation

Verbal communication, aptitude training, problem-solving skills, and soft skills development.

Year 2

Technical Foundations

Programming fundamentals, database concepts, networking basics, and technical interview preparation.

Year 3

Skill Transformation

Technical skill transformation, industry-specific training, mock interviews, and company-specific preparation.

Year 4

Placement Drive

Campus recruitment drives, dream company interviews, final placements, and career guidance.
