CURRICULUM

B.E. COMPUTER SCIENCE & ENGINEERING — R2021

The B.E. CSE program follows the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) with a total of 162 credits distributed across eight semesters. The curriculum is designed to provide a strong foundation in computer science fundamentals while offering flexibility through professional and open electives.

SEMESTER-WISE CREDIT DISTRIBUTION

162 Total Credits
Semester Credits Key Subjects
I22Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Python Programming, English
II26C Programming, Engineering Graphics, Basic Electrical & Electronics Engineering
III23Data Structures, Object-Oriented Programming, Data Science, Digital Principles
IV22Theory of Computation, AI & Machine Learning, Database Management, Algorithms, Operating Systems
V20Computer Networks, Compiler Design, Cryptography & Cyber Security, Distributed Computing, Electives
VI23Software Engineering, Embedded Systems & IoT, Electives (Professional/Open)
VII16Professional Electives, Open Electives, Management Elective, Summer Internship
VIII10Project Work / Internship
Total 162 Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)

CURRICULUM HIGHLIGHTS

Professional Electives

Offered in Semesters V and VI, categorized into specialization verticals including Data Science, Full Stack Development, and Cyber Security.

Open Electives

Students can choose open electives from other programs in Semesters VI, VII, and VIII to gain interdisciplinary knowledge.

Mandatory Internship

Summer internship in Semester VII and final project/internship in Semester VIII ensure industry readiness and practical exposure.

Value Added Courses

Industry-aligned certification courses in Cloud Computing, Full Stack, Cyber Security, AI, and Data Science through Centres of Excellence.
