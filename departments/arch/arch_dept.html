ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

The Department of Bachelor of Architecture was started with the sanctioned intake of 60 in the year 2008 and at present the annual intake is 240. The department aims at providing domain specific training and skills to the students for pursuing a career in the field of Architecture through state-of-the-art laboratory facilities and industry collaborations.

The department has state-of-the art laboratory facilities to fulfil the curriculum, Industry collaborated Centres of Excellence (COEs) and also to carry out various projects, research and consultancy activities. Additionally, we aim to equip our learners with the necessary skills to tackle global challenges, nurture an entrepreneurial and innovative mindset.
