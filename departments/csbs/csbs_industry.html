INDUSTRY COLLABORATIONS

The CSBS department collaborates with industry partners to provide students with exposure to real-world technologies and business practices. The curriculum is designed in consultation with IT industry experts to ensure alignment with current industry requirements.

The department organizes industry visits, guest lectures, and value-added courses featuring experts from leading technology companies. Students gain practical exposure through industry-sponsored projects, hackathons, and training programs.

Notable industry interactions include visits to Excerpt Technologies Pvt. Ltd., Bangalore, HTGE Technologies, and faculty visits to Systech Solutions, Bahwan CyberTek, Infosys, and CSIR.
