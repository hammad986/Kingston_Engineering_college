ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

The Department of Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS) was established to meet the growing demand for engineering talent with technology and business skills. The CSBS curriculum has been designed by eminent IT industry experts and academic professionals to integrate core computer science foundations with business management principles.

The department focuses on providing students with strong technical proficiency in computer science along with business acumen, data analytics capabilities, and entrepreneurial skills. Students gain hands-on experience through value-added courses such as Cloud Computing Technologies (Microsoft Azure, AWS), project-based learning, and industry collaborations.

The department organized a 5-day Value Added Course on Cloud Computing Technologies featuring industry experts from Finastra MISYS and Sonata Software Services, providing students with practical exposure to Azure AD RBAC and AWS cloud technologies. The department also actively participates in Project Expos, hackathons, and technical symposiums to nurture innovation and practical problem-solving skills among students.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Cloud Computing Training

Value Added Course on Microsoft Azure and AWS Cloud Technologies conducted with industry experts from Finastra and Sonata Software.

Business Systems Focus

Industry-designed curriculum integrating computer science with business analytics, data tools, and entrepreneurial skills.

Faculty Research

Faculty members actively publishing in international journals, book chapters, and attending FDPs on Data Science and Python programming.
