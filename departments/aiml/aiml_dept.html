ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

The Department of Computer Science Engineering with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI & ML) offers a cutting-edge program that integrates core computer science principles with specialized knowledge in artificial intelligence and machine learning. The curriculum is designed to equip students with the skills needed to develop intelligent systems, analyze complex data, and create innovative solutions using AI technologies.

The department focuses on providing students with a strong foundation in algorithms, data structures, programming, and mathematics, combined with specialized training in machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics. Students gain hands-on experience through project-based learning and industry collaborations.

The department has well-equipped computing laboratories and experienced faculty dedicated to nurturing the next generation of AI professionals and researchers.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

AI & ML Specialization

Specialized curriculum covering machine learning, deep learning, NLP, computer vision, and reinforcement learning.

Hands-on Learning

Project-based curriculum with real-world datasets, industry tools, and modern computing infrastructure.

Industry Connect

Collaborations with AtoS, NTT Data, LTIMindtree for Centres of Excellence and industry training.
