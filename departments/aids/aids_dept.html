ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

DEPARTMENT INTRODUCTION

The Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science was established in the year 2021 with an intake of 60 students. It is a popular outgrowth of computer science used to solve today's technologically complicated problems.

The department offers a UG programme leading to B.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, sensitizing students to execute intelligent data analysis and imparting knowledge of advanced technologies.

The department has cutting-edge laboratory infrastructure required to integrate the theoretical and practical facets of engineering, supported by well-trained and experienced faculty members in AI and data science.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

AI & ML Focus

Specialized curriculum focused on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science.

Cutting-Edge Labs

State-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure integrating theoretical and practical facets of engineering.

Expert Faculty

Well-trained and experienced faculty members dedicated to excellence in AI and data science education.

FOCUS AREAS & EXPERTISE

Generative AI & Machine Learning

Exploration of advanced algorithms, neural networks, predictive modeling, and the booming landscape of Generative Analytics.

Data Science & Business Analytics

Harnessing complex datasets to arrive at actionable foresight, insight, and hindsight for transformative decision-making.
