VISION AND MISSION

VISION OF THE DEPARTMENT

"Foster competent professionals by inculcating knowledge and skills in the Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning empire to cater needs of industry and society."

Source: AIDS Department Newsletter 2023-2024

MISSION OF THE DEPARTMENT

  • State-of-the-art Infrastructure: To provide a good environment with latest technological infrastructure facilities and interaction with industry in the field of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.
  • Problem Solving: To educate the students in problem solving with necessary skills to solve real world problems.
  • Experiential Learning: To strengthen the assimilation process of concepts in AI & ML through experiential learning.
  • Industry Integration: To create a better Academia-Industry liaison by means of skill enhanced training.
