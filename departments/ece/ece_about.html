ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering is committed to providing quality education in the areas of Electronics and Communication engineering to enhance the competitiveness of our graduates in the job market. The department focuses on developing a full-fledged centre for learning in recent trends of ECE, enabling graduates to excel as efficient engineers, managers, or entrepreneurs. With state-of-the-art infrastructure including a dedicated 4G/5G Laboratory established on March 7, 2024, the department fosters academic excellence along with research and innovation. The department encourages students to participate in hackathons, workshops, and inter-collegiate competitions, with notable achievements including the MSME IDEA HACKATHON 3.0 (Women) where the 'Smart Bike Safety Innovation' project received a financial assistance of Rs. 15 lakhs.

The department endows students with the knowledge, technical skills, and values that prepare them to excel as engineers and leaders in their profession. Through a combination of rigorous academic curriculum, practical laboratory sessions, and co-curricular activities, students gain comprehensive exposure to the latest trends in Electronics and Communication Engineering. The department promotes active learning, critical thinking, and engineering judgment coupled with business, management, and entrepreneurial skills to produce well-rounded professionals ready to meet global challenges. The department has been consistently achieving good placement records and academic excellence with students winning prizes in national-level technical symposiums, paper presentations, and sports tournaments.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

State-of-the-Art 4G/5G Lab

Dedicated 4G/5G Laboratory established on March 7, 2024, for academic and research purposes, providing hands-on experience with cutting-edge telecommunications technology.

MSME Hackathon Success

'Smart Bike Safety Innovation' project selected in MSME IDEA HACKATHON 3.0 (Women), receiving financial assistance of Rs. 15 lakhs — a testament to the department's innovation culture.

Excellent Student Achievements

ECE students have won 10+ prizes in national-level technical symposiums including paper presentations, coding competitions, circuit debugging, and sports tournaments such as Anna University Zone 6 Volleyball (First Place).

OBJECTIVES

To establish our department as a premiere center of educational excellence by providing a student-centered learning environment that is committed to changing the destinies of ordinary and disenfranchised persons so they can become extraordinary citizen leaders in their communities and the world.

Our department seeks to educate the whole person, and the great deal of learning takes place outside of the traditional classroom. Student life at our department is defined by the mixture and balance of academic rigor, co-curricular and extracurricular pursuits, and service to others.
