Kingston Engineering College offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes approved by AICTE and affiliated to Anna University, Chennai. Admissions are conducted as per the norms prescribed by the Government of Tamil Nadu and Anna University.

Programmes Offered

Kingston Engineering College offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes approved by AICTE and affiliated to Anna University, Chennai.

Four-year undergraduate engineering programmes affiliated to Anna University, Chennai.

B.E – Electronics and Communication Engineering

B.E – Mechanical Engineering

B.Tech – Information Technology

B.Tech – Artificial Intelligence and Data Science

B.E – Computer Science Engineering with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI&ML)

B.Tech – Computer Science and Business Systems

Two-year postgraduate programmes designed for specialized study and research.

M.E – Applied Electronics

M.E – Computer Science and Engineering

M.E – Engineering Design

M.E – Power System Engineering

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Eligibility

Admission eligibility criteria as per Tamil Nadu Government and Anna University norms.

Under Graduate Programmes

Admission Process: TNEA

Admissions to all U.G. courses (B.E. / B.Tech.) are made by Government of Tamil Nadu through TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions) administered by Anna University, Chennai.

For further details visit www.annauniv.edu.

Post Graduate Programmes

Admission Process: TANCET

Admissions to all P.G. courses (M.E., M.Tech., MBA, etc.) are made by Government of Tamil Nadu through TANCET (Tamil Nadu Common Entrance Test) administered by Anna University, Chennai.

Further details are available at www.annauniv.edu.

Research Programmes

Admission Process: Centre for Research, Anna University

Admissions to Ph.D. programmes are made by Centre for Research, Anna University, Chennai twice a year. Interested research scholars are expected to get in touch with the recognized supervisors and obtain their consent for being a supervisor to the applicant.

The details regarding the entrance tests for Ph.D. admission and other rules and regulations are available at www.annauniv.edu/research.

Cut-Off Marks

TNEA cut-off marks are published by Anna University each academic year. Please refer to the official TNEA website for the latest cut-off details for each programme and community category.

Cut-off marks vary each year based on factors such as number of applicants, seat availability, and reservation policies. Applicants are advised to check the official TNEA website (www.tneaonline.org) for the most current cut-off information.
