About NAAC 'A' Grade

NAAC (National Assessment & Accreditation Council) is an autonomous institution established by UGC to assess and accredit institutions of higher education. Kingston Engineering College has been awarded the prestigious 'A' Grade, placing it among the top-tier engineering colleges in Tamil Nadu — a recognition of our superior academic standards, infrastructure, research, governance, and student outcomes.
